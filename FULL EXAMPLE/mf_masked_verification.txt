PART I — Four-object identity (per principal direction):
======================================================================
  At each tau, the four scalar laws are functions of beta(tau):
    retention   = beta^2
    suppression = (1 - beta)^2
    leakage     = beta * (1 - beta)
    total       = 1 - beta
  Identity: total = suppression + leakage
  Max |total - suppression - leakage| = 1.04e-16 (machine precision)

PART II — Signal-side scaling predictions
======================================================================

  Predicted:  D_B(tau_0)^2 / D_A(tau_0)^2 = beta(tau_0)^2
  Empirical match (away from gap): max |error| = 6.66e-16
  This is the RETENTION scalar applied directly to the
  matched-filter signal-power loss.

Cell-C peak amplitude (mask-aware analyst, signal at tau_0):

  This is the cell-C signal-side attenuation factor;

======================================================================

through the same temporal mask M. The cross-correlation at
lag tau has variance proportional to A_M(tau), the auto-
correlation of the mask:
  predicted: Var[Chat(tau)] = sigma^4 * dt * A_M(tau)
  realizations: 4000

  (predicted: 1.235; mask geometry sets this)
  Empirical-vs-predicted Var max abs error: 5.8834e-05
  The bump in Cell-C variance at tau = 0 IS the phantom
  signature: an analyst applying a uniform threshold across
  lags would see preferential triggering at tau = 0 with
  NO physical signal present.
